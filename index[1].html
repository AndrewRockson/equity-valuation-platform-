01 / INVESTMENT THESIS

Exceptional business. Demanding valuation.

Microsoft delivered FY2026 revenue of $331.8B, up roughly 18%, and operating income of $155.2B, up roughly 21%. Azure surpassed $100B in annual revenue, reinforcing the strength of Microsoft's cloud and AI platform.

The valuation question is different from the business-quality question. Heavy infrastructure investment reduced FY2026 free cash flow to about $67.0B. Under the base-case assumptions in this project, both the DCF and peer cross-check indicate the market price requires more optimistic cash-flow growth and/or a lower required return.

MODEL CONCLUSIONSELL / AVOID AT CURRENT VALUATION

A valuation-driven conclusion, not a negative assessment of Microsoft's competitive position.
